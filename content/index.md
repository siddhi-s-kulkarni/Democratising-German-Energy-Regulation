## What this is

This is a personal, evolving knowledge base on German energy law and regulation written and explained in English built while I work through the legislative framework (EnWG, EEG, StromVKG), specific provisions, and how they apply across residential, commercial & industrial, and utility-scale market segments.

It is consciously and deliberately created in English since German energy law and regulation is exclusive to and dominated by the German language, making such topics relatively inaccessible to the growing international population working in the German energy industry. This project is my 2 cent effort in improving this accessibility. 

**This is not legal advice.** These are my own study notes and analysis, shared publicly because I think the structure is useful to others navigating the same material. I ask that you, the reader, always verify anything here against the official legislative text and guidance before relying on it.

**This is not an exhaustive list** of German energy regulation. It is an ever-growing and ever-evolving piece of work starting with regulation that I have most worked with.
