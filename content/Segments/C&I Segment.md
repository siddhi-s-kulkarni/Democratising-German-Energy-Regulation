Commercial and industrial consumers are in general those who have a higher load profile compared to home customers. These are usually #Behindthemeter customers however they can also have #Frontofthemeter installations.  

C&I customers can range from small commerce to very intensive industry size customers. For customers who have a yearly consumption > 100 MWh (100000 kWh) a #RLM is conducted, and they can pay their grid fee in their electricity bills according to a split #Arbeitspreis and #Leistungspreis model.  
Customers with a size smaller than this may be billed with a #standardloadprofile model or if connected at low voltage with qualifying #DERs / #Steuerbareverbrauchseinrichtungen  under [[§14a EnWG]] (note: certain commercial use heat pumps and AC units that are used for cold storage or critical infrastructure are excluded from this obligation).

The [[AgNes]] has a provision for customers with an RLM, that these must book a capacity and pay a #Leistungspreis based on this booked amount. If actual consumption exceeds the booked capacity in any 15 minute interval, the excess is booked at a higher #Arbeitspreis rate. This means that C&I customers have an incentive to book capacity carefully rather than simply booking based on historical maximum power consumption in a 15 minute interval.
