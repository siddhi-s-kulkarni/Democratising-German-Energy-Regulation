
The Home segment encompasses private homes as well as apartment buildings which are in the low-voltage grid in Germany.

A typical home customer is billed according to a #standardloadprofile (SLP) by the utility company. 

Over the past decades home customers have turned into prosumers (they generate their own electricity) through the installation of PV generation. Additionally, battery storage, EV charging and heat pumps are increasingly adopted by home customers as these become cheaper due to their economies of scale and to reduce dependency on price volatility.
Due to the rapid electrification of the transport and heating requirements, home customers increasingly require higher power supply which leads to congestion issues in the local grid. Furthermore, the high simultaneity factor of heat pumps and electric vehicle charging during the evening and night hours worsens this scenario. Therefore, in order to maintain system stability and at the same time provide incentives, the [[§14a EnWG]] regulation is applicable to such customers.
Home customers with [[§14a EnWG]] applicable #Steuerbareverbrauchseinrichtungen  are billed differently from a typical SLP customer. Rather than the flat grid fee structure, they can choose between the various grid fee models described in [[§14a EnWG]].

Additionally, since controllability of generation and the market participation of generation are also relevant to households, the [[§9 EEG]] and [[§19 EEG]] respectively are also extremely relevant to the home segment.
