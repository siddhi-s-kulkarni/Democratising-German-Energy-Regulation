This is a key law for the promotion of renewable energy generation in the German power sector. The Erneuerbare-Energien-Gesetz regulates the grid connection, acceptance and promotion of renewable energies. Since its introduction in 2000, it has been adapted several times to the changing conditions of the electricity market and the electricity grid.

[[§19 EEG]]
This deals with topics of #Einspeisevergütung and #Direktvermarktung. The relevant actors and things like who pays whom and value of #AnzulegenderWert, #Marktprämie and such is decided here.

[[§9 EEG]]
This deals with the concept of  controllability of generation plants. It decides from which size generation plants should be controllable by the grid operator and using which technology they should be controllable and what should therefore also be the extent of their controllability.
