The Energiewirtschaftsgesetz (EnWG) is a core law which regulates the German energy market (electricity and gas). It regulates non-discriminatory access to energy networks, ensures competition and sets framework conditions for using renewable energy.
It also regulates the tasks and responsibilities of several actors in the energy sector such as energy suppliers, distribution grid operators and consumers.

[[§14a EnWG]]
This paragraph decides on the controllability of #DERs via  distribution grid operators so that the grid operator can act in case of a congestion in the grid.
