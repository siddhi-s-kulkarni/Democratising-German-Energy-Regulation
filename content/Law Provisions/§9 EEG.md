This law regulates that generating plants should be controllable so that grid operators can curtail them in case the grid operator has to protect their grid such as in case of congestion or possible grid failures. §9 EEG applies broadly to generation plants (PV, wind, CHP and other renewable sources) but the specific thresholds and requirements are from the Solarspitzengesetz reforms and apply to PV plants specifically.

Earlier EEG versions only had rough requirements, but EEG 2012  brought the first requirements with regards to remote controllability. EEG 2012 brought a 70% feed-in cap for smaller plants unless remotely controllable. This rule was abolished in 2023, before being revived at a stricter 60% cap under the Solarspitzengesetz below.

Under the #Solarspitzengesetz , effective February 2025, plants above 7 kWp that receive EEG subsidies must be equipped with smart meter and control hardware (iMSys + Steuerbox) that enables remote controllability by the grid operator. Only systems below 7 kWp are exempt from this hardware mandate. However, systems below 7 kWp, along with the 7-25 kWp plants not yet equipped, are subject to the 60% feed-in limit until installation is complete.

The following table shows the applicable regulatory requirements for controllability based on plant size.

| **Plant size**               | **Remote controllability / Smart Meter**                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **> 100 kWp**                | **compulsory** ( #iMSys / RLM-Zähler & Fernsteuerbarkeit)                                                                                                                       |
| **25 – 100 kWp**             | **compulsory remote controllability and smart meter** (with #Steuerbox control box). A feed-in limit of 60% applies until installation                                          |
| **7 – 25 kWp**               | **compulsory remote controllability and smart meter** ( #iMSys+ #Steuerbox). A feed-in limit of 60% applies until installation (for new plants starting 25.02.2025).            |
| **2 – 7 kWp**                | no Smart Meter compulsory (except for charging points/heat pumps since 2024). **But:** for new plants starting 25.02.2025 without a smart meter a feed-in limit of 60% applies. |
| **< 2 kWp** (balcony plants) | no requirements (exempted from §9 EEG; max. 800 VA inverter power).                                                                                                             |
