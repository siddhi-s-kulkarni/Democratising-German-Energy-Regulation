Messstellenbetriebsgesetz (MsbG) has been in effect since 2016 and covers the equipping, metering and operation of electricity and gas. Only aspects relating to electricity are covered here for now. It states that all metering points have to be equipped with an #iMSyS by 2032 at the latest.

It is an important aspect of the digitalization of energy generation. The reason for equipping all metering points with an #iMSyS is to have a more detailed view of consumption and generation and therefore improve energy efficiency. Additionally precise generation and consumption data helps in planning for procurement of electricity which is necessary for ensuring security of supply.

Installation of #iMSyS is compulsory for consumers with consumption > 6000 kWh/year (mandatory since 2020, with rollout enforcement reinforced under a 2025 MsbG amendment), as well as for generators with an installed capacity > 7 kWp.

Every consumer has an assigned metering point operator and has the freedom to change their metering point operator.
